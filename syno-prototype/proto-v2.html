Rea Charalambidou

she/her · b. 1985, Limassol · Lives & works in Nicosia

Artist check_circleSelected · Open Call 2025

Formats

Sculpture Installation Mixed media Drawing

Artist statement

My practice is concerned with displaced bodies and forgotten gestures — fragments of memory carried across borders, stitched into objects, and re-presented as new artefacts. Working primarily with sculpture, installation and found materials, I treat the studio as a site of slow excavation, where personal histories meet collective archives.
