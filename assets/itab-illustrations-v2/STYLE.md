# iTAB illustration system v2

## Style anchor

`style-anchor-promo-envelope.png`

## Fixed visual rules

- Soft semi-transparent frosted hydrogel; airy rather than clay, wax, or opaque plastic.
- Broad blurred internal gradients with restrained pearlescent shimmer.
- 80–90% cool palette: cyan, clear blue, periwinkle, and soft violet.
- One small warm accent per illustration: golden yellow or pale tangerine.
- Rounded, readable silhouette with no more than 2–3 main visual groups.
- Front or gentle three-quarter view; consistent optical weight and generous padding.
- Transparent alpha background; no tile, frame, cast shadow, text, or watermark.
- Small icons must remain recognizable at 64–88 px; banner illustrations at 88–110 px.
