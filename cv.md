## Experience

### Product Designer — Hooh
**Jan 2025 — Mar 2026 · full-time, remote · AI document assistant, iOS and web · startup — the
only product designer, with a PM, product owner, art director and two engineers**

- Designed and shipped the MVP on web and iOS — upload, layered AI summaries, assistant chat and
  file organization. In internal testing every participant took a real document from upload to
  understanding unaided.

  markup and versioning: ten flows on one scaffold, designed in six weeks and handed off complete,
